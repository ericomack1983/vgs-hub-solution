Frequently Asked Questions

What is the VGS One-Stop Solutions Hub?

The VGS One-Stop Solutions Hub is an internal innovation platform where the Visa Government Solutions sales team can:

  • Explore live solution demos and blueprints
  • Interact with clickable mockups and MVPs
  • Simulate real-world government payment use cases
  • Identify client pain points before implementation
  • Validate assumptions during pre-sales engagements

It transforms static presentations into interactive solution discovery sessions — turning pitches into proof.

Who is this marketplace designed for?

Built for the VGS sales and solutions team, especially useful when engaging:

  • Government agencies and ministries
  • Enterprise and fintech clients
  • Pre-sales engineers and solution architects
  • Sales executives and account managers
  • Product and UX teams validating concepts
What problem does this solve?

Traditional pre-sales challenges include abstract diagrams clients struggle to visualize, misaligned expectations, and long sales cycles due to unclear value.

This marketplace solves that by enabling:

  • Real-time interaction with working prototypes
  • Scenario-based testing and flow simulation
  • Early pain-point discovery with the client
  • Faster executive alignment and shorter cycles
  • A clear "Show me, don't tell me" narrative
What does "POC Ready" mean on a card?

A POC Ready solution has a working prototype or MVP that can be demonstrated live to a client. It means:

  • The core flow is implemented and functional
  • A demo URL or working app is available
  • It's suitable for client-facing pre-sales sessions

It is not a production build — it's a validated proof of concept ready for client validation and scoped pilots.

What is a Solution Blueprint?

A Blueprint is a structured visual representation of the solution architecture, covering:

  • System architecture and component interactions
  • Business flows and API touchpoints
  • Compliance checkpoints and governance layers
  • Data movement and integration patterns

Think of it as the technical DNA of the solution — designed to align business and engineering stakeholders before a pilot begins.

How do I request a pilot or demo session?

Click "Try Demo" on any solution card to access the live prototype, or click "Request Demo" in the navigation to submit a formal pilot request. You'll receive a scoped plan with success criteria, a timeline, and an integration overview.

How does this accelerate the sales cycle?

The marketplace approach:

  • Aligns business and technical stakeholders early
  • Reduces ambiguity in requirements
  • Surfaces client objections before formal RFP
  • Quantifies operational risk up front
  • Demonstrates value in 30 minutes, not 30 slides
Can solutions be customized per client?

Yes. Each solution supports:

  • Industry-specific flows (government, fintech, NGOs)
  • Regional compliance overlays
  • Custom branding and API simulation layers
  • AI-driven decision logic adaptations
  • Multi-stakeholder role simulation

Sessions can be tailored to the client's business model and technical maturity level.

What types of use cases can be simulated?

Examples include:

  • Public fund disbursement and social transfer programs
  • Merchant and supplier onboarding journeys
  • Digital identity verification and KYC/KYB flows
  • Cross-border B2B payments and procurement
  • Tokenized asset flows and open-loop transit
  • AI-based eligibility validation and scoring
Is this environment secure?

All demos use synthetic datasets with no real production data. The environment is configured with:

  • Secure sandbox isolation
  • Controlled access roles
  • Compliance simulation (PCI, KYC, AML)

It is built for safe, risk-free client exploration.
